sort1 uses: Bubble Sort

How do you know?: The bubble sort will take a long time for any list that's out of order, however for the sorted group, bubble goes extremely fast.

sort2 uses: Merge Sort

How do you know?: Works the fastest for all sets except for sorted, which bubble sort was made for. Thus, the speediness of this sort is what makes it the merge.

sort3 uses: Selection Sort

How do you know?: The times for sort3 are all very similar and the average of the extremes, thus logically, the sort will always find the smallest number and move it to the front.
